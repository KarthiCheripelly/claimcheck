## What to read first
1. Master Circular – find the sections on pre-existing disease, waiting periods, moratorium, and claim rejection/grievance.
2. Policy wording V26 – find the "Exclusions" section (PED / specific waiting periods). Compare with V19.
3. 3–5 awards in Mediclaim Book14 that mention "pre-existing" – note why the complainant won or lost.
4. The two HC judgments (short – 3 and 6 pages).
